# Rhiannon AI, LLC

We're a small startup who believes privacy and cloud compute don't have to be at odds with one another - and no, the answer isn't Trusted Compute, or even Confidential Compute - [homomorphic encryption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homomorphic_encryption) is the answer to a large variety of problems that are centered around needing the compute power of fast machines in the cloud, to perform useful operations on public data, your own data, or data from 3rd party data provider (multi-key FHE, aka MK-FHE). Its not a silver bullet - and even with hardware accelerants like the powerhouse that the Alveo v80 is, or large, AI-sized clusters of Nvidia Blackwell cards, there are currently some computations that do yet have have a known, reasonably performing way to do under the promises of homomorphic encryption. But we're dedicated to taking calculated risks, not being afraid to embrace promising new technology like computable RAM (Samsung's recent demo of LPDDR5X-PIM, for instance), dedicated FHE accelerators using photonics so that physics can do most of the work for us (Optalysis's Computation in transit), and custom, exotic tape-outs of custom chips taking advantage of quantum-mechnical effects (notice we're note saying the words "quantum computer" - there are very real, differenet challenges involved).

What's all this good for today? We believe ourselves to be the first to offer a scalable, fully homomorphic and privacy-preserving encrypted vector search product.
